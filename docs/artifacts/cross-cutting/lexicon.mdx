<GovernanceArtifact
  title="Lexicon"
  gemaraType="Lexicon"
  layer="cross"
>

<Purpose>
Shared terms so catalogs, policies and logs use consistent language.
</Purpose>

<LayerRole>
Cross-cutting support for Layer 1 (and anything that cites lexicon terms). Definitions live beside guidance rather than inside measurement logs.
</LayerRole>

<WhyYouNeedThis>
Without a lexicon, “CLA”, “company-moderated”, and “sensitive commit” mean different things in guidance, threats and policy. Cross-references and audits become ambiguous.
</WhyYouNeedThis>

<Contains>

- **LexiconTerm** — `id`, `title`, `definition`
- Optional synonyms and external references per term

</Contains>

<KeyFields>

- `metadata.id`, `metadata.type: Lexicon`, `metadata.version`, `gemara-version`
- Term entries with stable ids used elsewhere by reference

</KeyFields>

<LinksUpstream>

- External glossaries, legal definitions, and organisational style guides (as citations on terms)

</LinksUpstream>

<LinksDownstream>

- [Principle](/docs/artifacts/layer-1/principle), [Guidance](/docs/artifacts/layer-1/guidance), and other catalogs may declare `metadata.lexicon`
- Humans and tools resolve wording consistently when reading controls and logs

</LinksDownstream>

<Example>

A FOSS contribution lexicon defines shared terms such as CLA variants and project categories used across guidance, policy and controls:

```yaml
terms:
  - id: cla
    title: Contributor License Agreement
    synonyms:
      - CLA
    definition: >
      A contract between the FOSS project and either an individual developer
      or the company contributing code. CLAs may be categorized as Individual
      CLAs or Corporate CLAs (CCLA).

  - id: corporate-cla
    title: Corporate CLA
    synonyms:
      - CCLA
    definition: >
      Provides direct corporate authority for employees of a company to make
      contributions with explicit authority for a patent grant in an open
      source license. Corporate CLAs must be signed by an authorized
      signatory to be valid.

  - id: company-moderated-project
    title: Company Moderated Project
    definition: >
      An open-source project initiated by the company where the company acts as
      the primary project moderator or maintainer.

  - id: company-interest-major-project
    title: Company Interest (Major) Project
    definition: >
      An open-source project consumed in company applications, moderated by a
      non-company entity, where the company wishes to influence project
      direction or contribute significant functionality.

  - id: personal-contribution-project
    title: Personal Contribution Project
    definition: >
      An open-source contribution effort conducted by employees outside regular
      company-contracted activities for projects that are not company moderated
      or company interest and provide no benefit to the company.
```

</Example>

<AntiPatterns>

- Defining terms only in prose PDFs with no stable ids
- Duplicating conflicting definitions inside every catalog
- Renaming terms without updating references

</AntiPatterns>

<Discussion>

A lexicon is small but high leverage. Gemara treats shared vocabulary as a first-class artifact so machine-readable governance does not silently diverge on everyday words. Keep terms short, stable, and cited where they come from outside the organisation.

</Discussion>
