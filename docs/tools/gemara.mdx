<Tool
  name="Gemara"
  homepage="https://gemara.openssf.org/"
  description="An activity-centred GRC model with typed, linkable governance artifacts across definition, activity and measurement layers."
>

<HelpsWith to="/docs/factors/govern-sensitive-activities" title="Govern Sensitive Activities">
Places sensitive activities between policy and evaluation so governance evaluates what actors are about to do.
</HelpsWith>

<HelpsWith to="/docs/factors/build-a-governance-twin" title="Build a Governance Twin">
Provides layered governance semantics and logs that a twin can bind to real resources and activities.
</HelpsWith>

<HelpsWith to="/docs/factors/governance-is-version-controlled" title="Governance is Version Controlled">
Publishes machine-readable schemas so catalogues, policies and logs can live as versioned artefacts.
</HelpsWith>

<HelpsWith to="/docs/factors/governance-is-composable" title="Governance Is Composable">
Supports mappings and layered artefacts so teams compose shared baselines without forking everything.
</HelpsWith>

<HelpsWith to="/docs/factors/context-in-decisions-out" title="Context In, Decisions Out">
Ties structured evaluation and enforcement logs to explicit requirement and plan ids.
</HelpsWith>

<HelpsWith to="/docs/factors/continuous-governance" title="Continuous Governance">
Measurement layers (evaluation, enforcement, audit) map continuous posture onto durable records.
</HelpsWith>

<HelpsWith to="/docs/factors/evidence-by-default" title="Evidence by Default">
Evaluation, enforcement and audit logs are first-class evidence artefacts rather than screenshots.
</HelpsWith>

</Tool>
