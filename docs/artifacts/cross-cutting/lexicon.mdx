<GovernanceArtifact
  title="Lexicon"
  gemaraType="Lexicon"
  layer="cross"
>

<Purpose>

A controlled vocabulary of shared terms so catalogs, policies and logs use
consistent language.

</Purpose>

<Role>

Without a lexicon, “CLA”, “company-moderated” and “sensitive commit” mean
different things in guidance, threats and policy. The lexicon is cross-cutting
support: definitions live beside Layer 1 artefacts and are referenced wherever
wording must stay unambiguous.

</Role>

<Examples>

<RevealItem title="Contributor License Agreement (CLA)" defaultOpen>

The FOSS contribution lexicon defines *CLA* as a contract between the project
and either an individual developer or the company, with synonyms and a clear
split into Individual CLA and Corporate CLA (CCLA). Legal review controls and
policy can then cite the same term.

</RevealItem>

<RevealItem title="Company-moderated project">

A defined project category used across guidance, applicability groups on
controls, and policy scope—so “moderated” is not a hallway synonym for “we care
about this repo.”

</RevealItem>

</Examples>

</LinksDownstream>

<AntiPatterns>

<RevealItem title="Definitions buried in one catalogue only">

Explaining “CCLA” only inside a control description. Other artefacts reinvent
the term and drift.

</RevealItem>

<RevealItem title="Lexicon as a novel">

Paragraph-length essays instead of crisp definitions. Terms should be citable,
not chapters.

</RevealItem>

</AntiPatterns>
