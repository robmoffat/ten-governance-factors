<GovernanceArtifact
  title="Threat"
  gemaraType="ThreatCatalog"
  layer={2}
>

<Purpose>
Catalog of threats: negative-impact opportunities tied to capabilities and pathways.
</Purpose>

<LayerRole>
Layer 2 — threats and controls. Threats specialise vectors onto capabilities and feed both controls and organisational risks.
</LayerRole>

<WhyYouNeedThis>
Controls without threats become generic checklists. Risks without threats cannot explain which technical opportunities create organisational exposure.
</WhyYouNeedThis>

<Contains>

- **Threat** — `id`, `title`, `description`, `group`
- Required links to capabilities; optional vectors and actors

</Contains>

<KeyFields>

- `capabilities` (required in schema practice)
- Optional `vectors`, `actors[]`
- `mapping-references` / imports for external taxonomies (e.g. FINOS OSR risks)

</KeyFields>

<LinksUpstream>

- [Capability](/docs/artifacts/layer-2/capability), [Vector](/docs/artifacts/layer-1/vector)
- External risk taxonomies via imports or mapping references

</LinksUpstream>

<LinksDownstream>

- [Control](/docs/artifacts/layer-2/control) mitigates threats
- [Risk](/docs/artifacts/layer-3/risk) materialises organisational risk from threats

</LinksDownstream>

<Example>

Threat of sensitive-data disclosure, linked to capabilities, vectors, and an external FINOS OSR risk category:

```yaml
threats:
  - id: sensitive-data-disclosure
    title: Disclosure of Sensitive or Non-Public Information
    description: >
      A contributor may publish confidential, proprietary, client, security,
      credential, architectural, or other non-public information in source
      code, documentation, comments, or community communications.
    group: information-disclosure
    capabilities:
      - reference-id: foss-contribution-capabilities
        entries:
          - reference-id: public-source-publication
          - reference-id: company-infrastructure-contribution
          - reference-id: community-engagement
      - reference-id: finos-osr-risks
        entries:
          - reference-id: data-leakage-risk
            remarks: >
              Aligns with FINOS OSR Data Leakage Risk for contribution context.
    vectors:
      - reference-id: foss-contribution-vectors
        entries:
          - reference-id: accidental-sensitive-commit
          - reference-id: unreviewed-public-release
    actors:
      - id: employee-contributor
        name: Company Employee Contributor
        type: Human
        description: >
          An employee contributing to a FOSS project from company or personal
          infrastructure.
```

</Example>

<AntiPatterns>

- Threats with no enabling capability
- Copying CVE lists wholesale as if they were organisational threats
- Threats that prescribe controls in their description

</AntiPatterns>

<Discussion>

Threats are the hinge of Layer 2: they look up at pathways and capabilities, and down at controls and risks. Keep them opportunity-shaped (“sensitive data can be disclosed via public publication”), not control-shaped.

</Discussion>
