<GovernanceArtifact
  title="Guidance"
  gemaraType="GuidanceCatalog"
  layer={1}
>

<Purpose>
Catalog of guideline statements adapted from human-readable policy into linkable entries.
</Purpose>

<LayerRole>
Layer 1 — definition. Guidance is the readable “what we expect” layer that controls later implement.
</LayerRole>

<WhyYouNeedThis>
PDF guidelines alone cannot be imported by policy, cited by controls, or proven in audits. Without a guidance catalog, Layer 2 controls float free of the narrative people actually read.
</WhyYouNeedThis>

<Contains>

- **Guideline** — `id`, `title`, `objective`, `state`, `group`
- **Statement** — atomic `text` under a guideline
- Optional rationale, exemptions, recommendations, applicability

</Contains>

<KeyFields>

- Links to [Principle](/docs/artifacts/layer-1/principle) and [Vector](/docs/artifacts/layer-1/vector) via multi-entry mappings
- `see-also` between guidelines
- Catalog `metadata.lexicon` when terms are shared

</KeyFields>

<LinksUpstream>

- [Principle](/docs/artifacts/layer-1/principle), [Vector](/docs/artifacts/layer-1/vector), [Lexicon](/docs/artifacts/cross-cutting/lexicon)
- Human-readable source documents (e.g. FOSS contribution guidelines)

</LinksUpstream>

<LinksDownstream>

- [Control](/docs/artifacts/layer-2/control) entries list guidelines they implement
- [Audit Log](/docs/artifacts/layer-7/audit-log) may cite guidelines as criteria

</LinksDownstream>

<Example>

Guidance for information classification before publication, with observable statements and linked vectors:

```yaml
guidelines:
  - id: foss-guidance-information-classification
    title: Information Classification for Publication
    group: compliance
    state: Active
    objective: >
      Review and approve company moderated and company interest contributions
      before transmitting material out of the company network.
    statements:
      - id: foss-guidance-information-classification.1
        text: >
          Contributions must be associated to an inventoried FOSS project
          registered in the OSPO FOSS inventory.
      - id: foss-guidance-information-classification.2
        text: >
          Contributions containing material non-public information, non-standard
          cryptography, or non-standard authentication mechanisms must be
          remediated before approval.
    vectors:
      - reference-id: foss-contribution-vectors
        entries:
          - reference-id: accidental-sensitive-commit
          - reference-id: unreviewed-public-release
```

</Example>

<AntiPatterns>

- Guidance that is secretly a control assessment requirement
- Unversioned wiki pages as the only guidance source of truth
- Guidelines with no ids, so controls cannot cite them

</AntiPatterns>

<Discussion>

Guidance bridges people and machines. Contributors still need readable expectations; Gemara insists those expectations also exist as entries with ids so controls and audits can point at them precisely.

</Discussion>
