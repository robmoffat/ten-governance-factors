<GovernanceArtifact
  title="Enforcement Log"
  gemaraType="EnforcementLog"
  layer={6}
>

<Purpose>

Prevention or remediation based on assessment findings.

</Purpose>

<Role>

Enforcement records what was done after evaluation: block a merge, open a
ticket, notify owners, require remediation. Methods should be those declared on
policy, justified by assessment results. Without an enforcement log, denials and
fixes exist only as chat noise or ephemeral CI annotations.

</Role>

<Examples>

<RevealItem title="Merge blocked after PR #417 secret-scan failure" defaultOpen>

When the evaluation log fails data-leakage checks, enforcement blocks the pull
request merge, posts a failing check run, and notifies the contributor and
OSPO—citing the policy enforcement method and the failed assessment as
justification.

</RevealItem>

<RevealItem title="Remediation notification, not only hard deny">

Enforcement can obligate follow-up (notify, ticket, re-run) rather than only
refuse. The log still records disposition, method and timing so audit can see
the closed loop.

</RevealItem>

</Examples>

<AntiPatterns>

<RevealItem title="Enforcement without evaluation">

Blocking or remediating with no cited assessment. You cannot show the action was
policy-justified.

</RevealItem>

<RevealItem title="Silent CI failure">

A red check that leaves no durable enforcement record. Weeks later, nobody can
prove what was blocked or why.

</RevealItem>

</AntiPatterns>
