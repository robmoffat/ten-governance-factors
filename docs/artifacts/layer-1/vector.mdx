<GovernanceArtifact
  title="Vector"
  gemaraType="VectorCatalog"
  layer={1}
>

<Purpose>
Catalog of contribution or attack pathways that inform guidance and threats.
</Purpose>

<LayerRole>
Layer 1 — definition. Vectors describe *how* harm can unfold before Layer 2 binds them to capabilities and threats.
</LayerRole>

<WhyYouNeedThis>
If pathways stay implicit (“people might leak stuff”), threats and controls cannot share a common attack story. Guidance also cannot point at the scenarios it is trying to prevent.
</WhyYouNeedThis>

<Contains>

- **Vector** — `id`, `title`, `description`, optional `group`, `applicability`

</Contains>

<KeyFields>

- Stable vector ids referenced by guidance and threats
- Catalog metadata and optional groups

</KeyFields>

<LinksUpstream>

- Incident history, threat intel, and operational anecdotes that name pathways

</LinksUpstream>

<LinksDownstream>

- [Guidance](/docs/artifacts/layer-1/guidance) may cite vectors
- [Threat](/docs/artifacts/layer-2/threat) entries often list vectors as attack paths

</LinksDownstream>

<Example>

Contribution pathways that can go wrong — sensitive commits, company-identity misuse, and ungoverned contribution:

```yaml
vectors:
  - id: accidental-sensitive-commit
    title: Accidental Commit of Sensitive Information
    group: publication
    description: >
      A contributor inadvertently includes credentials, client data, internal
      hostnames, security procedures, or other non-public information in a
      public commit, issue, or document.
    applicability:
      - company-moderated-projects
      - company-interest-major-projects
      - company-interest-minor-projects

  - id: company-identity-misuse
    title: Company Identity Misuse
    group: identity-and-infrastructure
    description: >
      A contributor uses a company-associated identity for personal activity,
      continues using a company email after employment ends, or fails to use
      the required company external identity for company contributions.

  - id: ungoverned-project-contribution
    title: Ungoverned Project Contribution
    group: governance-process
    description: >
      Contribution proceeds without required OSPO registration, approval, or
      inventory association.
```

</Example>

<AntiPatterns>

- Vectors that are really controls (“must run a scanner”)
- One vague vector that swallows every failure mode
- Vectors never linked from threats or guidance

</AntiPatterns>

<Discussion>

Vectors keep Layer 1 honest about failure modes without jumping straight to technology controls. They are the shared storyboard that threats specialise and guidance answers.

</Discussion>
