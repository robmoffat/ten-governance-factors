<GovernanceArtifact
  title="Risk"
  gemaraType="RiskCatalog"
  layer={3}
>

<Purpose>
Catalog of organisational risks linked to Layer 2 threats for policy-level treatment.
</Purpose>

<LayerRole>
Layer 3 — risk and policy. Risks translate technical threats into organisational language: severity, appetite, owners.
</LayerRole>

<WhyYouNeedThis>
Policy that only lists controls cannot explain *why* the organisation cares. Risk entries give mitigated/accepted treatment a named exposure with severity and ownership.
</WhyYouNeedThis>

<Contains>

- **Risk** — `id`, `title`, `description`, `severity`, optional impact
- Links to threats; optional owner / RACI
- Risk categories with appetite / max severity where used

</Contains>

<KeyFields>

- `threats` mappings into [Threat](/docs/artifacts/layer-2/threat)
- Severity and ownership fields for accountability
- Catalog metadata and groups

</KeyFields>

<LinksUpstream>

- [Threat](/docs/artifacts/layer-2/threat)

</LinksUpstream>

<LinksDownstream>

- [Policy](/docs/artifacts/layer-3/policy) `risks.mitigated` / `risks.accepted`
- Reporting and audit narratives that speak in risk language

</LinksDownstream>

<Example>

Organisational data-leakage risk aggregating the sensitive-data-disclosure threat, with owners:

```yaml
risks:
  - id: data-leakage
    title: Disclosure of Non-Public or Sensitive Information
    group: information-protection
    severity: High
    description: >
      A contributor may disclose confidential, proprietary, client, customer,
      security-sensitive, regulated, credential, key, architectural, or other
      non-public information through a public FOSS contribution.
    impact: >
      Could result in regulatory breach, client harm, security exposure,
      contractual breach, reputational damage, and loss of trust.
    threats:
      - reference-id: foss-contribution-threat-catalog
        entries:
          - reference-id: sensitive-data-disclosure
    owner:
      responsible:
        - name: FOSS Contributor
        - name: FOSS Project Administrator
      accountable:
        - name: Open Source Program Office
          affiliation: Company OSPO
```

</Example>

<AntiPatterns>

- Risks with no linked threats
- Treating risk catalogs as another control checklist
- Severity labels with no owner who can accept residual risk

</AntiPatterns>

<Discussion>

Risks are the organisational face of Layer 2. They should be few, named, and tied to threats—so policy can mitigate or accept them without inventing a parallel universe of concerns.

</Discussion>
